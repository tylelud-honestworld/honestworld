Integrity codes

The tricks are legal.
That doesn't make them honest.

Packaging is engineered to be believed, not read. These are three of the 23 integrity laws HonestWorld checks on every scan — with the kind of evidence the app shows you.

LAW 5−12 pts

Natural Fallacy

"Natural", eco and sustainability claims with no verifiable certification behind them. Words are free.

Front: "100% NATURAL"
Found: added flavour · no certifying body named
LAW 6−8 pts

Made-With Loophole

"Made with X" — but X is buried at the bottom of the ingredients list. Technically true at any amount.

Front: "made with REAL FRUIT"
Ingredients: fruit — position #7
LAW 7−10 pts

Serving Size Trick

A tiny declared serving makes every number on the panel look innocent. Nobody eats 19 grams of anything.

Per serving: "only 4g sugar"
Serving size: 19g — a quarter of a real portion

Questions answered

FAQ.

Everything people ask before their first scan.

Is HonestWorld free?
Yes. Unlimited barcode scanning, honesty scores, ingredient breakdowns and allergen alerts are free forever, with no ads. A Supporter upgrade inside the app unlocks unlimited photo label scans and extras — current pricing is always shown in the app on Google Play.
What does a scan actually show me?
An honesty score from 0 to 100, a clear verdict, every integrity law the label breaks, and the exact words on the packaging that broke them — plus a readable ingredient list, additives and allergen flags.
How are products scored?
Every product is checked against the same 23 integrity laws, covering ingredient order, claim accuracy, buzzwords, serving-size tricks and allergen disclosure. The same product always gets the same score. No sponsorship or brand relationship influences any score — none exist.
Is this a nutrition app?
No. HonestWorld doesn't judge nutrition or tell you what to eat. It judges honesty — the gap between what the front of the pack claims and what the label actually says. A product can be sugary and honest about it, or "natural" and misleading.
Which products does it cover?
Thousands of Australian supermarket products by instant barcode lookup, growing every day. Any product anywhere can be scanned by photographing its label.
What happens if a product isn't in the database?
Photograph the front and the ingredient list in the app. The product is analysed, scored, and joins the database — so the next person gets an instant result. The database grows with every scan.
Is it on iPhone?
HonestWorld is on Android via Google Play today. iOS is planned — email support@honestworldapp.com and you'll be notified the day it lands.
What data do you collect about me?
None that identifies you. No accounts, no ads, no tracking, no data selling. Your scan history stays on your device. The full details are in the privacy policy — it's short on purpose.
